Warehouse Management System

Purpose: View current inventory levels at warehouses. Monitor stock quantities, reorder levels, and status to ensure adequate supply for distribution to stores.

Note: This shows warehouse inventory (bulk stock for distribution). Store inventory (for customers) is shown in the Customer Service interface.

Low Stock Threshold: Products are marked as "Low Stock" when quantity ≤ Reorder Level. Each product has its own reorder level (shown in the table). Status: In Stock = Above reorder level, Low Stock = At or below reorder level, Out of Stock = Quantity is 0.

Note: Shipments are created when orders are actually shipped to customers. New orders appear in customer order history but don't automatically create shipments. Shipments are created separately when warehouse staff prepares and ships the order. This shows all shipments that have been created and tracked.

How it works: When new products arrive at the warehouse from vendors, use this form to update inventory. Enter the UPC, select warehouse, and quantity received. After updating, the new quantity will appear in the Inventory tab. The system automatically adds the received quantity to existing inventory.
