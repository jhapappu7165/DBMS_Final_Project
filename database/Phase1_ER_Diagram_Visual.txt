--------- RELATIONSHIP SUMMARY ------------

1. Vendor (1) ──< SuppliedBy >── (M) Product
   - Each product supplied by one vendor
   - Each vendor supplies many products

2. Product (M) ──< BelongsTo >── (M) Category
   - Many-to-many via ProductCategory junction table
   - Product can be in multiple categories
   - Category contains many products

3. Customer (1) ──< Places >── (M) Order
   - Customer can place many orders
   - Each order belongs to one customer

4. Store (1) ──< Processes >── (M) Order (optional)
   - Store processes in-store orders
   - Order.StoreID is nullable (for online/phone orders)

5. Order (1) ──< Contains >── (M) OrderItem
   - Order contains many items
   - Each item belongs to one order

6. Product (1) ──< OrderedAs >── (M) OrderItem
   - Product can appear in many order items
   - Each order item references one product

7. Order (1) ──< ShippedAs >── (1) Shipment
   - One-to-one: online order has one shipment
   - Shipment belongs to one order

8. Product (1) ──< StoredAt >── (M) Inventory
   - Product can have inventory at multiple locations
   - Each inventory record is for one product at one location

9. Inventory (1) ──< Triggers >── (M) Reorder
   - Low inventory triggers reorders
   - Multiple reorders possible per inventory location

10. Vendor (1) ──< Receives >── (M) Reorder
    - Vendor receives many reorder requests
    - Each reorder sent to one vendor



--------- CARDINALITY NOTATION --------------

(1) = One
(M) = Many
(1,1) = Exactly one (mandatory)
(0,1) = Zero or one (optional)
(1,M) = One to many
(M,M) = Many to many


---------- LEGENDS ---------------------------

PK = Primary Key
FK = Foreign Key
UNIQUE = Unique constraint
nullable = Can be NULL
NOT NULL = Required field
